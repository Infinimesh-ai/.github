# Infinimesh.ai

> **Your intelligence. Your control.**

We build local AI systems and intelligent hardware for individuals and teams. Our work brings together local compute, long-term memory, AI agents, and secure connectivity to keep your data and workflows under your control.

[Website](https://www.infinimesh.cn/) · [Explore Local AI](https://infini.dev/)
