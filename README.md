# Infinimesh.ai

Official site: [https://infinimesh.ai](https://infinimesh.ai)

Infinimesh.ai is building open, controllable AI infrastructure for teams and enterprises.  
We focus on making AI agents reliable, auditable, and production-ready through local-first deployment, governed workflows, and clear operational traces.

## What We Build

- **Local-first intelligent runtime** for running AI agents in controlled environments with low operational friction.
- **Tool-first integration** with safe, composable interfaces for enterprise systems.
- **Traceability and governance** to make important actions inspectable, reproducible, and auditable.
- **Approval-first workflows** for high-risk operations to improve safety and compliance.

## Open Projects

- [SparkClaw](https://github.com/Infinimesh-ai/SparkClaw): An AI runtime designed for high-performance local execution, with emphasis on stability, evaluation, and explainability.
- [LocalMind](https://github.com/Infinimesh-ai/LocalMind): A long-term direction for office and knowledge-work automation with auditable execution and robust task workflows.

Infinimesh.ai 专注于打造开放、可控且可持续交付的 AI 基础设施。  
我们希望让 AI 从“会回答”提升为“能稳定、可解释、可审计地落地执行”。

## 我们的方向

- **本地优先运行时**：在可控环境中部署和运行 AI 智能体，降低外部依赖风险。
- **工具化协同**：通过标准化工具接口，让模型与业务系统稳定联动。
- **可追踪治理**：为关键决策与动作保留完整审计链路，便于复盘与合规。
- **安全优先执行**：通过审批与权限机制，降低高风险动作带来的运营风险。

- [LocalMind](https://github.com/Infinimesh-ai/LocalMind)：面向办公与知识工作流的持续演进方向，面向可审计执行的任务自动化系统。
